A café that treats the street like a living room.

BABU started with a simple thought: good coffee shouldn’t be a special trip. It should be a few steps from your door, made well and priced for real life.

Why “BABU”?

A name that sounds like a nod, a tap on the glass, a hello.

“Quality street café” — that’s the whole promise in four words. We’re not a chain you pass through. We’re the steadier double-shot, the garlic rice that’s crisp at nine in the morning *and* nine at night, and the tall glass of something cold for when Naga turns up the heat.

The menu is built to be repeated: the Iced Spanish Latte you learn by name, the Spamsilog that shows up in review after review, the Mango Tapioca that reminds you summer comes around every single year.

01

Drinks that are actually good

Double shots, real milk — full-cream or oat — and recipes people come back for. Not a logo with a trend.

02

Meals that are meals

Silog with proper garlic fried rice and a sunny-side egg, sandwiches with real fillings, fries worth sharing.

03

Fair, street prices

Espresso from ₱170, a full silog from ₱199. A café shouldn’t need a special occasion.
