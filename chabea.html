Our Story

Chabea started as a quiet garden gap on Ateneo Avenue — now it's the alfresco table where half of Naga seems to end up on a slow afternoon.

We built Chabea around what the street was missing: a shaded, plant-lined space where you can order a proper cup of coffee, share a plate, and stay as long as the conversation runs. The dining room spills out into open-air seating under the trees, so the space changes with the hour — bright and busy at breakfast, low-lit and unhurried by evening.

Everything on the menu is built for lingering: coffee brewed to order, rice meals and pasta sized for sharing, and pastries that go fast on weekends.
